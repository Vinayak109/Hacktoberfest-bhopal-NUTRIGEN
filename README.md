# Hacktoberfest-bhopal-NUTRIGEN
NutriGen is a trigger-aware nutrition web app for people living with bloating, IBS and food intolerance. Users log what they eat and how they felt afterwards; NutriGen finds the ingredients that keep causing discomfort and builds trigger-safe Indian meal plans around them. Think of it as a food diary that learns your gut.
We call our users catalysts: every meal they log makes the plan sharper for them. NutriGen supports nutrition decisions; it is not a medical
diagnosis tool.

The problem we solve : People with digestive issues eat blindly. They know something hurts, but not which food. So they reach for antacids like Pudin Hara
(which treat tonight's bloat, not why it keeps coming back), cut foods on a hunch, or follow generic diets such as low-FODMAP that are
hard to stick to and do not fit everyone. IBS alone affects about 4.1% of the global population, yet few tools help people discover their
own triggers simply, in the food they actually eat.
